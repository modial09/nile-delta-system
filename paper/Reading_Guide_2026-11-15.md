## The five claims to check as you read

1. The guarantee ladder. An 80 percent floor is the highest level the model holds in every scenario. The exact ceiling is 80.25 percent. Holding the 80 percent level costs about 227.9 billion pounds of expected margin against the same model left free.
2. The historical pattern. The planting pattern Egypt actually uses holds no guarantee inside this model.
3. The stress split. Under a water cut, a profit only plan plants no staples at all, while the physical capacity to grow them stays high. The paper keeps capacity and choice apart everywhere.
4. The forecast verdict. No learning model beat the simple lines on any price series. The drift line won for every crop. Each forecast in the paper carries its backtest error beside it.
5. The water values. Water is priced as marginal values by governorate and by year type, not as one national average.

## Three places where your judgment is the test

1. Voice. Section 1 and Section 7 should sound like you wrote them. If a sentence sounds like a report about you, mark it.
2. The ceiling claim. Section 6.1 and the abstract call 80.25 percent a result under the stated construction, with its band printed. Check that the wording never reads as a law about the Delta itself.
3. The limits. Section 8 names what the model cannot do, including costs that stop at 2022 and a water budget that is a calibration. Check that nothing earlier in the paper claims more than Section 8 allows.
