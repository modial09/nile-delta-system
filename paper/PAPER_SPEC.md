# Paper specification, set by the founder on 14 November 2026

This paper will carry weight in the MIT MBAn application. It is written last, after the data foundation is real and the model has been rerun and reaudited on real data. Nothing is sent to anyone before the founder reads and approves the final text.

## What the paper is
An academic paper that explains the project: the problem in the Nile Delta, the model, what it found, and what a decision maker should do with it. It follows one narrative from the first page to the last: a fixed and shrinking water supply, a food promise that has a price, and a model that prices the choices.

## Voice
First person, as the founder. Plain simple words. Short clear sentences. Storytelling that a farmer, a planner and a professor can all follow. It must sound like a human analyst wrote it, because he did the work: no stock phrases, no inflated claims, no AI sound. The stance is that of a serious graduate analyst: every claim has a reason, every number has a source, every limit is stated where it belongs.

## Academic apparatus, required
1. Sources for every external fact and every dataset, cited in a references section with dates.
2. A data appendix: each parameter, its value, its source, its years, its unit.
3. A glossary appendix: every technical term used (dual variable, shadow price, stochastic program, scenario, guarantee) explained in one or two plain sentences for a reader new to the field.
4. A methods appendix: the model structure in enough detail that another analyst could rebuild it.
5. A sensitivity appendix: what moves the results and what does not.

## Novelty discipline
No claim of being first, only, or new in any form unless the prior work review proves the narrow claim and states its scope. The paper positions itself against the existing literature honestly: what others built, what they left open, and where this work adds something a decision maker can use. Value to the reader governs: the paper must be useful to the people who allocate water and to the farmers who live with the allocation.

## Assumptions
Any remaining assumption must be an educated guess with its reasoning shown: what it rests on, what range is plausible, and what happens to the results across that range. An assumption with no stated ground does not enter the paper.
