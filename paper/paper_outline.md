# Working paper outline

Working title: Allocating Water and Routing Fertilizer in the Nile Delta: An Integrated Stochastic Optimization Study

Target: SSRN working paper, with the code release and Zenodo DOI linked from the first page.

1. Introduction. The allocation habit problem, the cost of unpriced water, and the three contributions of the paper stated in one paragraph each.
2. Related work in brief. Hydro economic allocation models, stochastic programming in agriculture, and routing for input distribution, with the gap this paper fills stated plainly.
3. Data. Every source, its limits, and the cleaning decisions that matter.
4. Problem One. Formulation of the allocation model, the deterministic core, the baseline comparison against the real cropping pattern.
5. The forecasting module. Models, backtest design, accuracy results in full, and how forecast error becomes scenario spread.
6. The stochastic solution. Scenario results, the water value map, stress tests at reduced budgets, and the value of information measures.
7. Problem Two. Fertilizer demand derivation, the routing model, distance validation, and cost per ton against the nearest depot baseline.
8. Reality check. The stakeholder assumption audit and what it changed.
9. Limits. What the data cannot support, what the scenarios assume, and what a larger study would need.
10. Conclusion. The decision rules a planner or operator can take from the work, stated without inflation.
