# Data sources

Every entry states the source, what it provides, where it lives, and its pull status. Status values: to pull, pulled, cleaned.

1. FAOSTAT, Crops and Livestock Products domain. Provides Egyptian production, area harvested, and yield by crop and year. Status: pulled on 12 November 2026 through the Our World in Data grapher mirror, because the FAOSTAT site itself failed that day (the API returned server error 521 and the bulk download host returned 403). Files in data/raw named owid_{crop}-yields.csv and owid_{crop}-production.csv for wheat, rice, maize, sugar beet and potatoes, downloaded exactly as served. Egypt yield and production means over 2014 to 2023 are what version one uses. Cotton has no series in the mirror and is out of version one for that reason. A direct FAOSTAT pull is still owed to replace the mirror.
2. FAOSTAT, Prices domain, producer prices. Provides annual producer prices by crop for Egypt. Status: to pull. Partial anchor in hand: the wheat price in version one is the published government procurement price of 2,200 Egyptian pounds per ardeb of 150 kilograms for the 2025 season (USDA FAS Cairo Grain and Feed Annual and FAO GIEWS), which is 14,667 pounds per ton. All other prices in version one are declared illustrative values inside reported ranges.
3. FAOSTAT, Fertilizers by Nutrient domain. Provides fertilizer use by nutrient for Egypt. Status: to pull.
4. CAPMAS Egypt, agricultural statistics releases. Provides cropped area by governorate and crop, the district land base of the model. Status: to pull. Until it lands, version one uses an illustrative district land base for eight Delta governorates and a baseline pattern built from the real national crop mix, and the results note says so in plain words.
5. Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation, Egypt, published figures. Provides the Nile water quota and Delta irrigation context. Status: to pull. Version one calibrates its expected scenario budget just under the water demand of the current cropping pattern instead, and declares that choice in the code.
6. Published hydrology literature on Nile inflow variability. Provides the historical inflow range used to set the low, expected, and high scenarios. Status: to pull, candidate papers listed in the first data notebook. Version one scales the expected budget by 0.85 and 1.08 for the low and high scenarios and declares the probabilities as judgment.
7. Egyptian agronomy extension guidance and FAO publications. Provides fertilizer application rates per crop, the bridge from planted hectares to fertilizer demand. Status: to pull. The crop water requirements in version one are illustrative midpoints of published Delta ranges from the same family of sources, labelled illustrative in the code and in every printed result.
8. OpenStreetMap through the SCGraph world highways geograph. Provides the road network and pairwise distances for the routing layer. Status: available through the SCGraph package, no separate pull.
9. Egyptian fuel price, official announcements. Provides the diesel price parameter for delivery cost. Status: to pull at modelling time, with the announcement date recorded.
10. Truck operating cost per kilometre, published logistics sources. Provides the delivery cost parameter, declared as an assumption and stress tested. Status: to pull.

Rules: files land in `data/raw/` exactly as downloaded; cleaning happens only in code; if a source cannot be pulled, the gap is recorded here with the fallback the design names.
