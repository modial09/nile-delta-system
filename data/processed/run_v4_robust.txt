WaPOR values are total consumption over all surfaces per governorate,
with no cropland mask. They are never presented as cropland consumption.
The modeled figure covers the modeled crops only, on the applied basis,
while WaPOR measures consumption over every surface. No efficiency or
requirement scalar is derived from this comparison: direct calibration
is not defensible on these two bases, so WaPOR is used as validation only.
The modeled baseline demand sits below the WaPOR total in every
governorate, which is the order the two definitions require.
