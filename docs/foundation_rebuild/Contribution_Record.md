# Contribution Record: the Nile Delta Project

Mohamed Diallo, November 2026

This record states what I decided, built, checked, rejected and revised on this project, what AI assisted, and what the independent checks verified. It is drawn from the project files only. It invents no story and claims no endorsement.

## What I decided

**The design, 1 November 2026.** I set the project as one integrated piece: a two stage stochastic water allocation model for the Nile Delta as the core, with duality and shadow prices as the business layer; a machine learning forecasting module that produces next season price and yield forecasts per crop, with rolling origin backtests and errors reported in full; and a fertilizer routing model chained on top as a second layer only if the core was finished and written by early December. I killed the third option on the table at the same time, because modelled data cannot carry a readiness claim. The purpose was fixed from the start: demonstrate analytical capability, business analytics mindset and systems thinking, on a question a decision maker actually faces.

**The data rules, 14 November 2026.** When I rejected version 3 I set the rules the rebuild still runs under. Stale or missing data is found recent, or forecast from at least ten years of history with the forecast backtested and the error printed beside every use, or carried as a named gap with its cost stated. No silent carries. Arabic primary sources are a structural layer of the research, not a supplement. Satellite data is used where it is real. The paper is written last, from the audited run only.

**The scope cuts.** The combined foundation of 14 November 2026 took the routing layer out of the foundation, so it could not endanger the paper. The guarantee is reported as a frontier, priced level by level, never as a single chosen level. Information values are computed against true probability weighted means. Stress results always pair maximum staple capacity with the profit objective choice.

## What I built

I built the data foundation: the FAOSTAT price and crop series, the ministry cost bulletins read in Arabic through 2022, the district panel of eight governorates and five crops, the WaPOR satellite zonal statistics for 2018 to 2025, and the hundred year Aswan inflow record. I built the forecasting module that races ridge, random forest and gradient boosting models against naive and drift lines on rolling backtests. I built the allocation model through its versions, ending at version 5 with land binding by season against physical area, real abandonment decisions, quartile band layers, the drought state carried as a named stress outside the probability weights, and the capped ladder solved beside the relaxed one. The repository holds the code, the data, the run logs and the unrounded outputs for every version, and no earlier version was overwritten.

## What I checked

I commissioned and read an internal audit at every stage: the version 4 audit, where 38 of 45 headline entries survived; the reaudit of the repaired build, where 49 of 54 survived and none was withdrawn; and the audit of the written paper, where 60 of 64 entries survived and every fault found was wording around sound numbers. I brought in an external audit of version 3 from a separate model, which proved six defects my own audit had missed. Under the foundation rebuild of 15 November 2026 I commissioned two independent checks of the version 4.1 paper, one numerical and one domain critique, and then an independent verification of the version 5 build, followed by the two addendum runs that priced the ladder at scaled water anchors and completed the capped price list.

## What I rejected and what was withdrawn under my standard

I rejected version 3 as not sendable to any professor, on stated grounds: the value was not obvious to a reader, the computation was a textbook programme with none of the forecasting work I had designed, costs were old, satellite data was named and unused, and the mining had stopped early in English. The claims withdrawn across the versions, each replaced by a measured value or by silence, include the 52.92 percent gain of version 3 as an implementable result; the version 3 stochastic value figures computed against the wrong benchmark; the sentence that the historical pattern holds no guarantee at any level, which was false; the headline that the fixed plan holds its floor in 65.0 percent of a mixed sample; the version 4.1 penalised stochastic value of 7,607,863,594 pounds, withdrawn as a definition; the ceiling stated as anything more than a property of the stated model; and the audit side variants of the old basis that version 5 never recomputed.

## What I revised

On the audit evidence I revised the land formulation from an annual pool to seasonal pools against physical cultivated area; the stochastic benchmarks to true probability weighted means; crop failure to a real abandonment decision with water at zero and the planting cost sunk; the price vintage to the December 2025 FAOSTAT extract with a single 2025 forecast target year; the forecast bands to the residual quartiles so the layer weights and the construction agree; the drought object to a stress with no probability; and the reporting of robustness to two distributions, single years and ten year windows, never mixed.

## What AI assisted

The mining sweeps, the model builds, the audit passes, the verification reruns and the drafting of this record were executed with AI assistance, under my direction and my rules. The external audit of version 3 was produced by a separate AI model and supplied by me to the project. AI assistance is stated here because the record should show it, and because the decisions, the rejections and the standard the work is held to are mine.

## What the independent checks verified

The independent verification of version 5, run by a checker who did not build it, reran the whole build in a scratch copy and regenerated the saved outputs exactly; recomputed every headline figure with separate arithmetic; and replayed the floor 80 problem and the ceiling problem under a different solver, with agreement to 3,206 pounds on 291.1 billion for the floor and to a third of a tonne for the ceiling. The addendum runs add three more agreements of the same kind. The numerical half of the acceptance gate is met on that evidence.

## What nobody has verified

No Egyptian irrigation or agricultural specialist has reviewed any version of this work, and no computation substitutes for that review. No professor has seen the paper. It has been sent to no one, and it goes to no one before I have read it and approved it. Until the specialist review and the band calibration decision have happened, the work carries its stated label: a numerically verified exploratory model study on a stated basis.
