# S3 Gate A self assessment for the version 5 build

Stage S3 of the foundation rebuild, written 15 November 2026 by the repair lead. This is a self assessment: the author of the repairs grading his own build against Gate A of the handoff in docs/handoff_2026-11-15. It is not the independent verification Gate A also requires, and it does not pretend to be.

## The gate, in the handoff words

Gate A: the model may support validated empirical claims only after material physical and economic consistency checks and independent numerical verification pass. An honest illustrative case remains deliverable with appropriate labels.

## Verdict

The version 5 results may not yet be called validated empirical results under Gate A. They may be called what they are: a repaired, internally consistent exploratory model study, whose proved defects are closed, whose remaining weaknesses are named and in two cases measured, and whose numbers await the independent numerical verification that Gate A requires and that has not yet been run on this build. The recommended label for every version 5 figure, until that verification passes, is the label the version 5 results note already carries by construction: model results on a stated basis, with their conditions printed beside them.

## What the repairs closed

The two failures of Check 1 are closed on consistent definitions. The historical pattern sentence is replaced by a measured ladder test: the pattern holds the 50, 60 and 65 percent levels in every scenario and fails from 70 percent. The penalised VSS is now the difference of two quantities computed under one definition, 75,931,039,486 pounds, and the inconsistent figure is withdrawn in the register.

The S2 findings inside the repair order are closed. The price layer rests on the current vintage, with 2024 observed where it exists and the target year aligned at 2025 for prices and yields together (finding 1 and the target year half of finding 2). The band convention contradiction is closed: bands are residual quartiles and the weights match them (finding 5). The drought severity reading is closed by removing the state from the simplex (finding 4). The Menoufia fill pairing is closed (finding 6), and the twelve loader actions on blank cells are on the printed record (finding 7, and S1 defect item 4). Headroom precision and the dual floor label are closed (S1 defect item 5, Check 1 checks 5 and 8 in their presentation half). Headline results are saved unrounded as structured files (S1 defect item 10). The robustness reading is closed as a reporting matter (Check 2 item 3.4).

Properties the handoff S4 list requires were observed in the repaired runs, on the author side: the frontier is strictly decreasing in the floor at every level and under every variant; scenario probabilities sum to one over nine scenarios; the ceiling binds feasibility rather than solver status, with 85 percent infeasible by the ceiling maximisation and not by a status string alone; capacity and choice are printed side by side in every stress run; the true mean budget is the genuine probability weighted mean, 11.0000; the standard VSS is withheld because its benchmark plan is infeasible under the floor; relaxation duals are labelled as relaxation duals with the integer objective printed beside them at the same floor.

## What remains open, in the order it binds

1. Independent numerical verification of version 5 has not been run. Check 1 verified version 4.1 and found two failures that three earlier audits had missed, which is the strongest evidence in this project that author side agreement is not verification. Until a Check 1 style pass recomputes the version 5 frontier, pattern test, information values and duals from the saved code and inputs, Gate A cannot pass, whatever the repairs closed. This item alone decides the verdict.

2. Band coverage is measured and weak. Sequential coverage of the quartile band runs from 0.0 to 22.2 percent for prices against a construction value of 50 percent, because the pooled backtest residuals are dominated by the low price era. The convention is now consistent, but the uncertainty the layers carry is thinner than the current price regime exhibits. Every expected money figure inherits that thinness. The hard floor, ceiling and feasibility results do not depend on the layer weights and are untouched by this item. Closing it needs a stated calibration choice, for example bands built on recent residuals only, with its own backtest printed; that choice was outside the repair order and is not smuggled in here.

3. The water anchor is still a calibration. Every budget scales from 11.00 billion cubic metres, a number no source publishes, split by land share. Check 2 asked for the ladder and the ceiling repriced at scaled anchors of 0.90 and 1.10; the stress runs scale budgets for capacity and choice, but the priced ladder at a scaled anchor still does not exist. The guarantee prices therefore carry an unmeasured sensitivity to the single most consequential physical choice in the build.

4. The cost gap is priced, not closed. Costs remain official 2022 values against 2025 prices. The bracketing runs at times 1.50 and times 2.00 show the cost of the 80 percent guarantee moving from 268,610,886,052 to 224,549,654,133 and 180,141,961,977 pounds, with the ladder order unchanged. Those factors are labelled assumptions, because no measured factor exists in the sources held. A reader who needs a single year margin still does not have one.

5. Smaller named items stand open. The winter potato cost is an assumption standing in for a gap in the bulletin. The rice response factor of 1.20 is carried labelled unverified, with the ceiling insensitive to it at 1.00 and 1.40. The seasonal pool approximation against the real crop calendar, named by Check 2 item 1.1, is still not stated in the paper text. Water and abandonment respond to the full scenario including the yield layer, the mild information leak of Check 2 item 3.2, still without its one sentence of acknowledgment in the paper. No Egyptian irrigation or agricultural specialist has reviewed any version of this work; a cross vendor model critique, if one is ever run, supplements that absence and does not replace it.

## What would change this verdict

Two events, both executable with what the project already holds. First, an independent verification pass on version 5, run from the saved code, the corrected prices extract and the saved inputs, testing the S4 property list and recomputing the headline table; if it passes, the numerical half of Gate A is met. Second, a decision on the band calibration, taken in the open: either rebuild the layers on a stated recent window and print the new coverage, or print the coverage shortfall as a standing limitation beside every expected figure. With those two events, and with the anchor sensitivity of item 3 run and printed, the repaired results could be presented as empirical model results with named conditions. Without them, the honest label is the one this assessment gives: a repaired exploratory study, stronger than the paper it succeeds, and not yet validated.
