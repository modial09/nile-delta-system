# Gate A final statement for version 5 as amended by the version 5.1 addendum

Written 15 November 2026 by the version 5.1 addendum lead. This statement consolidates the claim labels for version 5 as amended. It rests on four records and nothing else: paper/Results_Note_v5.md, docs/foundation_rebuild/CheckV5_Independent_Verification.md, docs/foundation_rebuild/S3_Gate_A_Assessment.md, and the two addendum runs saved as data/processed/run_v5_1_scaled_anchor.txt with data/processed/results_v5_1_scaled_anchor.json, and data/processed/run_v5_1_caps.txt with data/processed/results_v5_1_caps.json, both produced by src/allocation_model_v5_1_addendum.py calling the version 5 model unchanged. No version 5 file and no version 5 input was changed by the addendum.

## The verdict

Version 5 as amended may be called a numerically verified exploratory model study on a stated basis. It may not yet be called validated empirical results, and no figure from it may be worded as a finding about Egypt itself. The numerical half of Gate A is met. The empirical half stays closed for the reasons in the blocked table below, and this statement is the label that governs every version 5 figure until the two closing events named at the end have happened.

## The verification and its two caveats, stated plainly

The independent verification passed. The verifier rebuilt nothing, reran the whole build in a scratch copy, regenerated the saved outputs exactly, recomputed every headline figure with separate arithmetic, and replayed the floor 80 problem and the ceiling problem under HiGHS, a different solver from the CBC the build used, with agreement to 3,206 pounds on 291.1 billion for the floor and to a third of a tonne for the ceiling. The addendum runs add three more agreements of the same kind. The anchor column of addendum Run 1 reproduces data/processed/results_v5_main.json exactly at every rung, and its 65 percent rung, never solved by the build itself, returns 363,687,244,833 pounds, equal to the verifier own fresh solve at that floor. The scaled rung 0 margins and ceilings of Run 1 reproduce the version 5 stress figures printed in Section 6 of the results note. Addendum Run 2 reproduces data/processed/results_v5_caps.json at every shared rung and the capped ceiling share exactly.

Caveat one. The floor 80 plan holds its floor within solver tolerance, not exactly. Imposed as a hard constraint on the saved plan, the floor returns Infeasible in the three low layer scenarios where the plan is tight. Clearance maximisation, which asks how much staple tonnage the fixed plan can hold in each scenario, returns 9,803,521.000 tonnes against a floor of 9,803,520.985 tonnes in those scenarios, so the plan can hold its floor with 0.015 tonnes to spare, and easing the floor by a single tonne makes all nine scenario solves return Optimal. The guarantee of the saved plans is therefore real, and it is established by maximisation, and it is exactly as thin as that sentence says. A second part of the same caveat: the floor lives in the plan together with its recourse. Replaying the saved floor 80 planting with margin seeking recourse and no floor imposed delivers a minimum scenario tonnage of 8,926,542 tonnes, below the floor. Anyone handed the plan files must also impose the floor holding recourse to reproduce the guarantee.

Caveat two. The price and yield layer probabilities are nominal. The bands are built exactly as stated, at the quartiles of the backtest residuals, and the layer weights of 0.25, 0.50 and 0.25 describe that same construction. But measured sequential coverage of the price bands runs from 0.0 percent for wheat to 22.2 percent for potatoes against a construction value of 50 percent, because the pooled residual history is dominated by the low price era. The layers therefore understate the spread of the current price regime, and every expected money figure inherits that thinness. The hard floor, ceiling and feasibility results do not depend on the layer weights and are untouched by this caveat. One label inside the same construction also misleads: both residual quartiles for potato prices are positive, so the low price layer for potatoes, 10,876.83 pounds per tonne, sits above the expected layer of 10,753.27. The arithmetic is right and the word low is wrong for that one series.

The full ladder and the ceiling were solved at total anchors of 9.90 and 8.80 billion cubic metres, the version 5 anchor scaled to 0.90 and to 0.80, beside the ladder at the anchor itself. The ceiling moves with the anchor: 82.41 percent of the reference tonnage at 11.00 billion cubic metres (10,099,349 tonnes), 75.86 percent at 9.90 (9,295,584 tonnes), and 69.30 percent at 8.80 (8,491,820 tonnes). Rungs above the ceiling of their own anchor are infeasible by that ceiling maximisation, not by solver status alone. The reading is three sentences. A tenth less water removes the top rung of the ladder entirely, and the dearest surviving rung at 9.90, the 75 percent guarantee at a cost of 263,919,142,021 pounds, costs within 5 billion pounds of what the 80 percent guarantee costs at the full anchor. A fifth less water removes everything above 65 percent, and the 65 percent guarantee then costs 226,520,935,005 pounds, more than the 70 percent guarantee costs at the full anchor. At every surviving rung the margin falls and the cost rises as the anchor falls, with no exception in either table of this statement. The anchor remains unmeasured. What Run 1 measures is the price of being wrong about it, and that price is now part of the record wherever the ladder is quoted.

## Addendum Run 2: the capped price list

The uncapped frontier prices the guarantee inside a relaxed world, as the results note itself says, because its optima plant potatoes at up to several hundred times their observed areas at fixed prices. The implementable companion is the same ladder under the named rotation and market sanity caps carried from version 4.1: wheat 0.50, rice 0.35, maize 0.40 in each season, sugar beet 0.30 and potatoes 0.15 of district physical land in each season. The version 5 caps mode solved most rungs but never solved the 65 percent rung and saved no cost column. Run 2 completes the list on the version 5 build. Margins in pounds; the cost at each rung is taken against the capped unconstrained optimum of 294,692,290,467 pounds.

The capped ceiling is 79.50 percent of the reference tonnage (9,742,281 tonnes), so the capped ladder tops out at 75 percent, one rung below the relaxed ladder. The reading is two sentences. Inside the caps, a guarantee is far cheaper at every shared rung: the 75 percent guarantee costs 32,666,817,585 pounds capped against 242,759,240,394 pounds in the relaxed world, and even the first rung costs only 3,011,807,538 pounds. This capped list, complete at every rung with its cost column, is the price list a decision reader should be handed; the relaxed frontier of the results note stands beside it as the price of the same promise when planting is allowed to leave the observed pattern without limit.

## Claims that may be carried, as verified model results on a stated basis

Each claim below may be carried with its basis sentence attached. The basis is part of the claim; a claim separated from its basis is no longer the verified claim.

1. The guarantee frontier at the 11.00 billion cubic metre anchor, on 2025 forecast prices and official 2022 costs with published district yields: unconstrained expected margin 559,713,476,348 pounds, falling strictly rung by rung to 291,102,590,296 pounds at the 80 percent floor, a cost of 268,610,886,052 pounds against the unconstrained optimum of the same build, with every rung above 80 percent infeasible. Carried subject to caveat one on tightness and caveat two on every expected figure.
2. The ceiling: no plan on this basis holds more than 82.41 percent of the reference tonnage of 12,254,401 tonnes in every scenario, and the ceiling statement is a statement about this model with its budgets, duties, yields and land formulation, never a statement about what the Delta itself cannot do.
3. The historical pattern test: the pattern Egypt actually plants holds the 50, 60 and 65 percent ladder levels in every scenario under staple maximising recourse and fails from 70 percent, with a worst scenario capacity of 8,056,850 tonnes. The version 4.1 sentence that the pattern holds no guarantee at any level is withdrawn and must not be quoted.
4. The information values at the 80 percent level: EVPI of 13,458,470,835 pounds on comparable sets, and a penalised value of the stochastic solution of 75,931,039,486 pounds on the one consistent penalised definition, penalty 25,000 pounds per tonne of shortfall on both sides. No standard VSS exists for this benchmark, because the true mean plan cannot hold the floor in 7 of the 9 scenarios, and none may be reported.
5. The robustness of the fixed 80 percent plan, reported as two distributions and never mixed: on 50 single year samples the plan holds its floor in 46.0 percent of draws with a worst decile mean shortfall of 1,911,850 tonnes; on 50 ten year window samples it holds in 48.0 percent with a worst decile mean shortfall of 497,301 tonnes. Neither figure is an annual reliability, and the weaker single year statement is the true headline.
6. The water value table, labelled exactly as the build labels it: one optimal dual solution of the LP relaxation at the exact floor of 0.8231405524 as a share of the reference tonnage, with the relaxation objective of 273,356,788,636 pounds printed beside the mixed integer objective of 272,969,293,877 pounds at the same floor, a gap of 0.142 percent. Dual solutions are not unique.
7. The forecasts and their errors as computed values: drift selected for every price series, the 2025 point forecasts and quartile bands as printed in Section 11 of the results note, the backtest errors beside them, the measured band coverage figures, and the recorded paddy rice event, in which the ridge model won the backtest and then diverged on the full data refit and drift was selected in its place under a stated rule.
8. The stress pairs, capacity and choice always side by side: at budgets scaled to 0.90 a maximum staple capacity of 9,295,584 tonnes while the profit objective plants no staples at a margin of 520,907,656,409 pounds; at 0.80 a capacity of 8,491,820 tonnes while the profit objective plants no staples at 476,620,802,204 pounds; and the drought stress at its own budget of 9.580 billion cubic metres, which carries no probability and enters no expected value.
9. The ladder at scaled anchors, from addendum Run 1, as the measured sensitivity of every guarantee price to the water anchor, quoted with its anchor in the same sentence as its price.
10. The capped price list, from addendum Run 2, as the implementable companion to the relaxed frontier: capped margins from 294,692,290,467 pounds unconstrained to 262,025,472,882 pounds at the 75 percent floor, a capped ceiling of 79.50 percent, and the 80 percent rung infeasible under caps.

## Claims blocked from empirical policy wording, with the blocker for each

1. Any sentence that states what the Delta, or Egypt, can guarantee, should plant, or would gain. Blocker: the water anchor is unmeasured. Every budget scales from a calibration of 11.00 billion cubic metres that no source publishes, split across governorates by physical land share. Run 1 bounds the damage a wrong anchor does and does not reduce it: the anchor itself remains a calibration, so every result is a result about the model on its stated basis.
2. Any sentence that states a margin or a guarantee cost as the figure for a single real year, including the sentence that the 80 percent guarantee costs 268,610,886,052 pounds in 2025. Blocker: the money basis is mixed. Prices are 2025 forecasts and costs are official 2022 values, bridged only by assumed escalation factors of 1.50 and 2.00, under which the cost of the same guarantee moves to 224,549,654,133 and 180,141,961,977 pounds. No official cost table after 2022 exists in the sources the project holds, so no single year margin exists to quote.
3. The uncapped frontier quoted on its own as the price list for a decision. Blocker: fixed price potato expansion in the uncapped optima. The unconstrained optimum plants winter potatoes at 16.91 times their observed area, summer potatoes at 22.71 times and Nili potatoes at 369.63 times, and plants no staples at all; at the 80 percent floor the multiples are still 2.81, 6.81 and 108.91 times. At those multiples the fixed prices stop being credible. The frontier may travel only with its market absorption warnings attached and with the capped price list of Run 2 beside it.
4. Any sentence that reads a robustness figure as the chance a real season keeps the promise, or that calls the fixed plan reliable. Blocker: the recourse in this model sees the full scenario, including the yield layer, before water and abandonment respond, an information advantage over any real planting season whose size is unmeasured. The robustness figures are evaluations of one fixed plan under staple maximising recourse with its late season planting frozen at the normal inflow variant. They describe that plan inside this model and nothing about a real year.
5. Any sentence that quotes a water value as a price, a tariff, or the marginal value of water in a governorate. Blocker: the printed values are duals of the LP relaxation of a model whose keep and abandon decisions are discrete. They are one optimal dual solution, conditional on the scenario, at one stated floor. They are not marginal values of the mixed integer model, as the finite increments on the full model were never the printed object, and they are not prices.
6. Any sentence that presents an expected margin as a measured expectation about the coming season rather than as the output of the stated scenario weights. Blocker: the layer probabilities are nominal, per caveat two. Measured band coverage for prices runs from 0.0 to 22.2 percent against a construction value of 50 percent, so the weights claim a spread the recent price record does not support.
7. The label validated empirical results itself, in any wording, for any version 5 figure. Blocker: no Egyptian irrigation or agricultural specialist has reviewed any version of this work. Independent numerical verification, which this project now has, checks that the arithmetic is the arithmetic of the stated model. It cannot check that the stated model is the right model of the Delta, and no cross vendor model critique substitutes for that review.

## What the addendum changed, and what still closes the gate

The addendum closed the two run shaped gaps in the record. The ladder now stands priced at three anchors, so the sensitivity the verifier and the self assessment both named as open is measured and printed. The capped price list now stands complete at every rung with its cost column, so the implementable companion the paper central product needs exists in full. Neither run touches a blocked claim: every blocker in the table above survives the addendum, because none of them is a missing run.

Two events still close Gate A, in this order. First, the band calibration decision, taken in the open: either the layers are rebuilt on a stated recent window with the new coverage printed, or the coverage shortfall is printed as a standing limitation beside every expected figure, as this statement already prints it. Second, a human review by an Egyptian irrigation or agricultural specialist, of the model as much as of the numbers. Until both events have happened, the label of this statement governs: a numerically verified exploratory model study on a stated basis, its proved defects closed, its remaining weaknesses named, and in the cases of the water anchor and the band coverage, now measured.
